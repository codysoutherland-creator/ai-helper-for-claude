Version: 0.8.0
Date: 2026-11-13
  Features:
    - The autopilot plans by material balance: it compares iron and copper plates made against plates used, finds the
      plate holding science back, and adds a smelting cell for it after the electric drills are in.
    - Defence before the first attack: gun turrets and stone walls are researched once red science runs on a map with
      nests, sooner when a nest is near or polluted. The mall makes firearm magazines, turret groups get wall arcs,
      and nests the pollution cloud is about to reach are defended first.
    - Radar: once the Radar tech is in, a radar goes up at the hub, and more go on the edge as the base grows,
      limited by the power available.
    - Blue science: a block with sulfur, engines and advanced circuits off the bus, fed by the oil plant, plus chained
      labs that research blue techs.
    - Robots: batteries through to robotics, a robot mall (frames, construction robots, roboports, chests), a roboport
      grid over the base, and the helper leaves ghosts inside a robot network to the construction robots.
  Bugfixes:
    - The helper can now tear down chests and belts in its way before their stage's other ghosts are built.
    - Plates lying on the ground no longer count as obstacles when a stage is checked.
    - The helper stops mining ore by hand when the plates it needs have already come in.

Version: 0.7.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Features:
    - Defence. When biters destroy something of yours you get a chat alert with a map pin, a ghost takes its place and
      the helper rebuilds it. Gun turrets are checked every minute and the helper brings firearm magazines (from
      chests, or crafted from iron) to any holding fewer than 10.
    - The autopilot defends: after an attack with no turret near it, gun turrets go to the front of the research
      queue, then a group of 4 turrets is placed facing the attack; nests the pollution cloud reaches get a group on
      the side of the base facing them.
    - Claude in chat: factorio_defense (attacks, turrets, ammo, nests and their pollution) and factorio_defend (place
      a turret group).
    - New settings: "Defence: rebuild what biters destroy" and "Defence: keep gun turrets in ammo" (both on).
  Bugfixes:
    - Mining outpost blueprint: the west drill column had no power (poles on both sides now).
    - Fast travel no longer lands the helper on the tile it is about to build on ("can't place ... blocked").
    - Autopilot: stages of one master plan no longer mark each other replaced; a stage being built is not offered
      again; later stages are offered while an earlier one still has a few unplaceable ghosts.
    - Plans tab sorted by what you need to do; chat shows the time of each message, no cost line.

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-11-05
  Features:
    - "Let Claude play" (autopilot): the host switches it on in the Helper tab, with @claude autopilot on|off|status
      or "let claude play". Claude then plays by itself: it restores power and fuel, keeps research queued, builds the
      starter base stage by stage, and keeps growing it. A status line shows the goal and the next step.
    - Growth past the starter base: a mall right after the electric drills (belts, inserters,
      undergrounds, pipes, steam engines, electric drills, assemblers, fast and long-handed inserters and iron chests
      made by assemblers, each into a limited chest; fed by its own taps on the starter base's plate lines); more
      steam power in the reserved room; a straight plate bus with smelting cells at the ore, a steel column and a
      second red + green science block on it (downstream of the mall, which keeps the first pick); then oil:
      pumpjacks on the nearest crude oil, a refinery on basic oil processing and a chemical plant making plastic.
    - The helper takes building materials from the mall first, waits at a mall machine that is making the item
      rather than crafting, and once a mall exists hand-crafts at most 120 crafting seconds per 10 game minutes
      (short crafts count too; an item the mall makes only gets a short top-up). The hand-craft share shows on the
      status line; fuel fetched for burners is counted apart.
    - Expansion feeds stay short and straight (at most 1.4x the straight distance + 12 tiles, 6 turns), so no new belt snakes
      across the map. When everything it can build is built it says so once and idles instead of retrying.
    - In-place swaps (burner drills -> electric drills) gather the replacements before taking anything apart, so
      production never stops halfway through the swap.
  Changes:
    - Autopilot research skips weapons in a peaceful game and never queues a tech whose science packs the base does
      not make.
    - New map settings: allow removal, keep-out radius, grace period, player items, Claude calls per hour.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Starter-base planner v3: the whole starter base is designed first (through automated red and green science),
      then split into stages that only add to it or swap in place: S0 burner iron + coal, S1 burner copper, S2 steam
      power + labs, S3 automated red science, S4 electric drills in the burner drills' slots, S5 green science.
    - Burner drills sit in the slots of the electric drills that replace them; poles, belts and the steam block are
      reserved from the start. Later stages are drawn as faint outlines in the preview.
    - The later stages wait in the Plans tab (Roadmap) and are offered automatically when their research is done and
      the stage before them is built ("Stage ready: ...").
    - Green science block: cables, circuits, gears, inserters and belts from their own assemblers, 0.5 packs/s.
    - Variants differ in topology: a central hub (furnaces and science in one block; each ore field sends its ore
      and coal on one belt) or smelting at each ore field (one plate belt per metal, split for green science at S5;
      the columns' far side keeps plate chests for building). The recommended one says why.
    - Nothing built in an early stage is ever taken up again: the only removals are in-place swaps (burner drill ->
      electric drill, burner inserter -> inserter, a temporary chest -> the belt that replaces it).
    - S3 builds 10 labs, S5 16, so the labs use the 30 red (and 30 green) packs/min the assemblers make.
    - Plans tab roadmap rows show each later stage's build time, what it needs and what it replaces.
  Changes:
    - Your idle builds are only taken apart, and coal only taken out of your idle machines, when you allow it
      (planner option allow_removal, off by default). New belts may still cut an idle belt line where they cross
      it; the card lists those tiles.
    - Smelting-column burner inserters get 10 coal per fill (one ran dry right after the first stage).
    - The helper's message says what its round really does (hand-fed burners vs only restarting coal fields); belt-fed
      boilers of a planner v3 stage are no longer refuelled by hand.
    - From steam power on, every smelting-column inserter is electric (burner inserters moving ore ran out of fuel).
    - Approving a stage whose belt crosses one of your idle belt lines takes out just the crossed tiles.
    - Helper: primes coal fields and restarts one that stopped; stores salvage in a chest of its own when its
      inventory is full; fetches start fuel for every batch of burners it places.
    - place_ghosts keeps the input/output half of underground belts.

Version: 0.4.1
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Starter-base planner v2: plans from your actual state (inventories, chests, machines, production, research).
    - Variants are different builds: Quick start (burner pairs on the iron patch, no belts), Balanced (iron and copper
      on their patches), Long-term (hub only when its belts are short, otherwise grow on site) and Rebuild.
    - Every plan card shows the bill (needs / have / salvage / short), ETA to first plates and full rate, plates/min,
      belt length, footprint, a one-line trade-off and quality warnings.
    - Rebuild variant: can take apart or convert specific old builds when that clearly pays off. It lists exactly
      what it removes and why; the preview draws them in red.
    - Approve marks exactly the listed entities for deconstruction; the helper dismantles them, keeps the items and
      then builds. Reject or Dismiss cancels the marks that are left. Player ghosts are never touched.
    - Each variant carries a checked stage 2 on the same footprint: steam power at the water, power for idle labs or
      new labs, copper pairs.
    - Helper: takes coal out of idle machines to start new burners, and keeps hand-fed burners fuelled from the
      plan's coal drills (refuel round).
  Changes:
    - The planner no longer proposes a central hub with very long ore belts; long runs are flagged.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - New map setting "Who can use Claude" (host only / host and admins / everyone). Default: host only.
  Changes:
    - Public name: AI Helper (for Claude). The internal mod name stays claude-bridge, so existing saves keep working.
    - In multiplayer, only players allowed by "Who can use Claude" can ask Claude, use the helper buttons and chat
      commands, clear Claude's ghosts or act on plans. Others get a free one-line explanation.
    - Running or cancelling a held (expensive) Claude job and approving a plan always need the host or an admin.
    - Test and cheat ops (simulated chat, clicks and confirmations, instant build, give items) now need the new
      startup setting "Debug: enable test and cheat ops" (off by default).
    - Deconstruction requests over the local link are limited to a radius of 64 tiles.
  Bugfixes:
    - Incomplete multi-part link requests are capped (16 open, 1 MB) so they can't bloat the save.
    - Map export folder names are sanitized.
